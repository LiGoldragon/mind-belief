A picture enters as a picture through the typed prompt, in Claude and in Codex, and at the bottom stratum when a tool returns it or the flow opens the file. Entry files, hooks, skills, briefs and subflow reports carry text only; there a picture is a path the model opens. The top stratum takes a picture only in Codex, as a developer-role item injected into the thread.

A picture binds weakly: contrary text at any stratum wins. A line at the middle stratum or above that names a picture as the rule makes the model follow the picture when it opens it, through every way tried except a Claude hook.

Claude drops a picture at compaction; Codex keeps a typed one. Until then the picture is paid for on every turn: about 300 tokens at 700×140 pixels, about 3,000 at 1500×1500.

For a model, text is the cheaper form of a figure. Mermaid or code carries structure and labels in 165 to 250 tokens, where a picture 1000 pixels wide costs 1,100 to 2,100; colour is written into the Mermaid with classDef and linkStyle, for about 100 tokens more. A picture wins per token only at 180 to 250 pixels wide, where Sonnet still reads it and Haiku begins to fail. A legend that gives a colour an arbitrary meaning is obeyed.

Measured on 21 November 2026 with Haiku, Sonnet and a cheap Codex model, a few runs each.
