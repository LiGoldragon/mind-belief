---
description: "The visual source and the drawn page: how a source is written for the visualizer, which of its units are drawn, and what the visualizer hands the visual publisher."
dependencies: [notion:visual-publisher:0]
---

A source opens with the Presentation name, a PascalCase expression and its revision, and its creator's signature. A section heading is its module struct, such as `{ Vision protos 0 }`. The section's units come from the module's lines; author comments stay in the source. The units follow, separated by blank lines: a paragraph, a fence of code or a chart. A unit whose lines begin with + comes in; one whose lines begin with - goes out; a unit going out followed by one coming in is a unit replaced.

A picture is kept beside the source, with the unit it was drawn from and its readings. A unit that stands as it was keeps its picture; a source given again has only its changed units drawn.

A fence of code has its picture, and the code itself goes on the page with it. A chart has its picture, and its source stays off the page: the page carries code only for a fence of Ethos, Datom or another language of the system.

The visualizer records the model that drew the page in the manifest. It writes a VisualPage datom with the Presentation name, the signature formed from the manifest's creator and drawer, and each unit with its picture, change and word count. It sends the drawn page's path to the visual publisher as an Order.
