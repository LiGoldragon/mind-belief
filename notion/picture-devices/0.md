# The devices of a picture

A picture shows a flow: things, and what passes or happens between them. These are the devices it is built from; each line is a device and what it shows.

Arrow: one thing acts on another, or goes to it. Its label is the verb, or the name of what travels.
Order: top to bottom, or left to right, is first to last.
Number: steps marked 1, 2, 3 are taken in that order.
Ghost: the same thing drawn twice, the earlier one faded: it moved from there to here.
Before and after: the same thing side by side, the changed part marked: it became this.
Fade: what is past, leaving or not yet.
Dashed line: what may happen, or is not yet built.
Thick and thin: the thick line is the main path, the thin one the side path.
Branch: one arrow splitting, each branch labelled with its case: a choice.
Merge: several arrows into one thing: gathered.
Loop: an arrow returning to an earlier thing: again, until.
Gate: a bar across a path that lets some through: a check.
Enclosure: a frame around things: they are one thing, or inside it.
Nesting: a thing drawn inside another: the outer holds the inner.
Lane: a column for each actor; a step stands in the lane of the one who does it.
Nearness: things close together belong together.
Row: alike things in a line, alike in size and form: they are of one kind.
Stack: one thing drawn several times, overlapping: many of it.
Size: the larger thing matters more, or is more.
Bar: a quantity drawn as a length, beside its number.
Highlight: one thing bright among dim ones: this is what the picture is about.
Colour: one colour, one meaning; a thing keeps its colour everywhere.
The thing itself: a file drawn as a file, a person as a person, a page as a page.
Plus and cross: a plus or a green margin, coming in; a cross, a strike or a red margin, going out.
Magnifier: a part drawn again, larger, beside the whole: look closer here.
Real values: the boxes hold an actual case, a real name, a real line, so the idea is seen in an instance.
Callout: a short word joined by a thin line to the thing it names.
