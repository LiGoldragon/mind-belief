A string is drawn as the source writes it: bare when it is bare, with no guillemets; in its guillemets only when the source has them.

Draw the delimiters large and in one ink; their shape says the construct. The Quality column records the construct's quality; drawing colour comes only from the nature of the thing drawn. Its ground is neutral unless it has a nature, shown as shares of colour by notion:planetary-visuals:0. The same holds for each field, chip and nested thing.
