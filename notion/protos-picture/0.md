A string is drawn as the source writes it: bare when it is bare, with no guillemets; in its guillemets only when the source has them.

Draw the delimiters large; their shape says the construct. Square brackets surround an enum declaration; an enum value is its word alone on its ground. A vector value in Datom keeps its single square brackets; white square brackets belong to an Ethos vector type declaration. The Quality column records the construct's quality. A delimiter wears its type's nature colours, or ink when its type has no nature. Its ground wears the value's nature in colour shares by notion:planetary-visuals:0, or stays neutral when the value has no nature. The same holds for each field, chip and nested thing.
