## Drawing a chart

```datom
[ DescribedVisualObject.{ Nature «the heaviest tendency gives a thing its colour and its shape; the next gives its edge; the weights say how much of each» }
  DescribedVisualObject.{ Act «an arrow in the colour of its own nature, its verb on it» }
  DescribedVisualObject.{ Yes «a red arrow, no word» }
  DescribedVisualObject.{ No «a blue arrow, no word» }
  DescribedVisualObject.{ BothWays «one arrow with a head at each end» }
  DescribedVisualObject.{ May «a dashed arrow» }
  DescribedVisualObject.{ Statement «the largest words in the picture» } ]
```
